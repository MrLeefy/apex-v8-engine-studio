# 2006 Chevrolet Tahoe 5.3L Vortec 5300 — Engine Assembly Manifest

Target application: GMT800 2006 Chevrolet Tahoe, defaulting to the L59 (VIN 8th digit Z, FlexFuel) used in the Tahoe family. The LM7 (VIN T) uses the same core Gen III 5.3L iron-block geometry. This manifest deliberately replaces the previous fictional DOHC twin-turbo architecture.

## Factory geometry encoded in the model

- 90-degree V8, 5,328 cc / 325 CID
- Bore: 96.0–96.018 mm (3.780 in nominal)
- Stroke: 92.0 mm / 3.622 in
- Crank throw radius: 1.811 in
- Bore spacing: 4.400 in
- Deck height: 9.235–9.245 in (9.240 nominal)
- Cylinder bank longitudinal offset: 0.9488 in, left/driver bank forward
- Connecting rod center-to-center length: 6.098 in
- Main journal diameter: 2.559 in nominal
- Rod journal diameter: 2.0995 in nominal
- Main housing bore: 2.751 in
- Hydraulic roller lifter diameter: 0.842 in
- Rocker ratio: 1.7:1
- Compression ratio: 9.5:1
- Gen III 24X crank reluctor
- Firing order: 1-8-7-2-6-5-4-3
- Cylinder numbering: driver/left 1-3-5-7 front-to-rear; passenger/right 2-4-6-8 front-to-rear

## Modeling rule

A checked item means it exists as an explicit mesh, repeated mesh set, or mechanically meaningful grouped representation in the rebuilt Three.js engine. Tiny production hardware such as individual wire-terminal crimps, microscopic spring keepers, casting flash, sealant beads, stamped supplier marks, and every production bolt's exact head marking are not claimed as OEM scan data. The model uses published factory/service dimensions where available and mechanically faithful procedural geometry elsewhere; it does not falsely claim access to GM proprietary CAD solids.
